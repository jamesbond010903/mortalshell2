![Mortal Shell II achievements and trophies](/images/steam-07.jpg)

The trophy count and names below are drawn from the public achievement and trophy list. Unlock conditions are still being confirmed post-launch, so treat specific steps as community-reported until verified in-game.

Mortal Shell II has 53 trophies and achievements in total: 1 Platinum, 1 Gold, 10 Silver, and 41 Bronze. The same list is shared across PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.

Most trophies unlock naturally as you progress through the campaign, collect Shells and weapons, and defeat bosses. The only long tail is clean-up: all Tarstones, all Ova, all Map Fragments, all Lute Tracks, and all Shell Memories.

## Trophy breakdown

The Platinum trophy is “No Lifer”, which unlocks after every other trophy. The single Gold trophy is “This is Not an Achievement” for completing the game. The 10 Silver trophies are collection and progression milestones: all Ova, all Lute Tracks, all Tarstones, max bonding tier for a Shell, a maxed weapon at the Tarforge, all Map Fragments, all Shell Memories, all Shells, all Sidearms, and all Weapons.

## Boss trophies

Each major boss drops a Bronze trophy on defeat. They cover Magdalena, the Lady of the Woods; The Lost Child; The Nameless Captive; Sir Isaac, the Scholar-Prince; Hexapod; The Monolith; Droeg, the Conquerer; Malborn Offspring; Orrem, the Reclaimed; and Zmey, the Unbidden.
