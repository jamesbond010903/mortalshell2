Mortal Shell II launches on Xbox Series X|S on August 20, 2026, alongside PS5 and PC. Xbox players can buy the Standard or Devout Edition from the Microsoft Store.

At launch the game is not on Xbox Game Pass, so Xbox players need to purchase it separately. See the [Game Pass](/game-pass) page for details.

## Xbox editions and pricing

The Standard Edition is $49.99, and the Devout Edition ($59.99) adds up to 72 hours of early access and the Obsidian skin set. In Brazil, pricing is R$ 159 for Standard and R$ 189.99 for Devout.

## Xbox Game Pass status

Mortal Shell II has not been announced for Xbox Game Pass as of launch. The first Mortal Shell eventually joined the service, so a future Game Pass release is possible but unconfirmed.
