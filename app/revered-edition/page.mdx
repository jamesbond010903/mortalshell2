The Mortal Shell II Revered Edition is the PS5 physical collector's edition, priced at $69.99. It bundles the base game with a set of physical collectibles.

The Revered Edition is a physical release and is reported to have sold out at some retailers, so availability is unconfirmed by region.

## What the Revered Edition includes

The Revered Edition contains the base game, a SteelBook case, an art book, fine art prints, and a collector's box. It also includes bonus skins: the Obsidian set and the Skeletal Harbinger skin.

## Revered Edition vs other editions

Where the [Devout Edition](/devout-edition) is digital and focused on early access, the Revered Edition is physical and focused on collectibles. It does not add early access on top of the base game, so check whether early access matters to you before choosing.
