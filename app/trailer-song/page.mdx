Two songs feature across the Mortal Shell II trailers. The Summer Game Fest 2025 reveal trailer used “Ben Sahar” by Behemoth, and the later release date trailer used “Darker Thoughts” by Paradise Lost.

Both are metal tracks chosen to match the game's dark fantasy atmosphere.

## Summer Game Fest reveal song

The Summer Game Fest 2025 reveal trailer featured “Ben Sahar” by Behemoth, from the album The Satanist.

## Release date trailer song

The release date trailer used “Darker Thoughts” by the British metal band Paradise Lost.

## Where to hear the trailers

The official trailers are available on the Mortal Shell YouTube channel and the official website. The release date trailer is also linked from this wiki's home page under “Official media.”
