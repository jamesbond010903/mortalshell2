Mortal Shell II releases on August 20, 2026 for PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. It is developed by Cold Symmetry and published by Playstack.

## Platforms and launch date

The game launches simultaneously on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S on August 20, 2026. The standard edition retails for $49.99 / €49.99 / £39.99, with the Steam China price at ¥198.

## Early access window

Early access is tied to the Devout Edition, which includes up to 72 hours of playtime ahead of the standard launch. That puts the early access start at August 17, 2026.

## What to expect at launch

Launch content spans 8 playable Shells, 60+ dungeons, and a compact interconnected open world built around fast, high-risk combat. The full game's quest runs roughly 25-30 hours.
