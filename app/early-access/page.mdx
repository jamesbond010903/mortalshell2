![Mortal Shell II early access](/images/steam-12.jpg)

Mortal Shell II early access (also called Advanced Access) starts on August 17, 2026, up to 72 hours before the standard August 20 launch. It is included with the [Devout Edition](/devout-edition).

Early access is not a separate beta: it is the full game made available early to Devout Edition owners.

## How to get early access

Buy the Devout Edition (the digital deluxe version) to unlock up to 72 hours of early access. The Devout Edition is priced at $59.99 / €59.99 / £47.99.

## What early access includes

Early access grants the full base game ahead of launch, plus the Obsidian skin set for all eight playable Shells. Progress carries into the full release, so there is no downside to starting early.
