The Mortal Shell II Devout Edition is the digital deluxe version of the game. Its headline bonus is up to 72 hours of [early access](/early-access), starting August 17, 2026.

It is priced at $59.99 / €59.99 / £47.99, with the Steam China price at ¥228.

## What the Devout Edition includes

The Devout Edition bundles the base game, up to 72 hours of early access, and the exclusive Obsidian skin set for all eight playable Shells.

## Devout Edition vs Standard

Compared to the $49.99 Standard Edition, the Devout Edition adds the early access window and the Obsidian skins. If you plan to play on day one of early access, the Devout Edition is the only way in. For the physical collector's version, see the [Revered Edition](/revered-edition).
